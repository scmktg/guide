Chocolate
Factory Gosford

A working chocolate and nougat factory in West Gosford where Bon Bon, Nougat Limar and Sydney Marshmallow Co share one deliciously fragrant roof.

Visit website

Behind the glass, the good stuff is being made.

This is the sweetest detour on the Central Coast and probably the most fragrant. Three artisan makers share the West Gosford site, so you can watch glossy chocolates being enrobed, slabs of nougat sliced and pillowy marshmallow set, all on the same visit.

Build your own chocolate bar at the counter, graze a tasting tray with the kids or stock up on gifts you have no intention of sharing. Perfect for a rainy morning or the back end of a beach day.

Guest perk

A complimentary build-your-own chocolate bar with any in-store purchase over twenty dollars.
