About

The right hat, fitted properly, for the rest of your life.

Coastal Hatters is owned by Eric, a master hatter best known for many years at Sydney's Strand Hatters, and it shows. The store carries more than three thousand hats, with a strong lean toward Australian-made names like Akubra, Cancer Council and Canopy Bay.

Walk in for a free expert fitting, ask for a reshape on a hat you already own, or leave with a panama that finally suits your face. It is the antidote to the petrol-station bucket hat.
