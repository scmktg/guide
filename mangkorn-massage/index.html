About

Where pressure heals, stillness speaks.

Mangkorn, the Thai word for dragon, sets the tone here: power, grace and deep renewal. Trained therapists work through acupressure, rhythmic compression and assisted stretching to release tension and restore flow, with oil, hot stone, foot and cupping options on the menu.

The studio is quiet, warm and unhurried, with couples suites for paired bookings and short foot-focus sessions for the time-poor. It is an easy reset between beach days and dinner reservations.
