See the Coast from the air.

Operating out of Warnervale Airport since the early 1970s, the Central Coast Aero Club is the Coast's home of light aviation. Scenic joy flights take in the Tuggerah Lakes, Sydney Harbour and the Hunter Valley, while the school trains everyone from first-timers up to commercial pilots.

Trial Instructional Flights put you at the controls within minutes of arrival, and the uncontrolled airfield means very little time spent waiting. It is the holiday memory that puts the rest of the trip into perspective.
