01. About the hotel

A waterfront icon at the heart of the Central Coast.

The Beachcomber Hotel & Resort, affectionately known to locals as 'The Beachie', is a long-standing Central Coast landmark reimagined as a boutique waterfront destination. Sitting directly on the edge of Tuggerah Lake in the seaside town of Toukley, it pairs sweeping water views with a breezy Hamptons-inspired aesthetic of timber, rattan and soft coastal tones. The Beachcomber forms part of the Ascend Hotel Collection and is one of the most loved meeting places on this stretch of coast.

Accommodation ranges from comfortable hotel rooms through to spacious suites and family-friendly apartments, all designed with a relaxed beach-house feel. Guests have access to a resort-style pool club, lush garden areas, complimentary on-site parking and easy strolls to Toukley's beaches, lake foreshore, cafes and boutiques. Buffet breakfast is served daily, and the friendly team can help guide you to the very best of the Central Coast.

Two on-site venues take care of dining: Pelicans Restaurant offers a refined, seafood-led menu in a light-filled lakeside setting, while Beachie Bar and Bistro turns out wood-fired pizzas, elevated pub classics and tropical cocktails with a casual, lively vibe. The resort also features multiple bars, the pool club, conference and event spaces, secure parking and beautifully landscaped grounds — everything you need for a weekend escape or a longer Central Coast stay.
