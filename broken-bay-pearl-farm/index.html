About

A working pearl farm, an hour from the city.

Twenty thousand oysters drift below the surface of Broken Bay, each one quietly growing a single Akoya pearl. Every tour ends with a live harvest — a pearl lifted from its shell for the first time, still wet from the river.

Tours leave daily from our shoreside cellar door at Mooney Mooney. Bring a hat. The light off the water is unreasonable.
