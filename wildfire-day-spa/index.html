Wildfire
Day Spa

A luxurious destination spa inside Kooindah Waters Resort, with magnesium float pool, infrared sauna and couples suites set among golf-green fairways.

Book

A proper Central Coast reset.

Wildfire Day Spa is tucked inside the Mercure Kooindah Waters Resort, with treatment rooms designed to feel a long way from the everyday. Therapists work with Bioline luxury skincare across massages, facials, body scrubs, soaks and full beauty services.

Two immersive couples suites, a magnesium float pool and an infrared sauna make it the kind of half-day you build the rest of the holiday around. It is unhurried, considered and quietly indulgent.

Guest perk

A complimentary float pool session with any 60-minute treatment.
