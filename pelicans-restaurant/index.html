About

Coastal-modern dining at The Beachie.

Pelicans Restaurant is the signature dining room at The Beachcomber Hotel & Resort, set in an airy, light-filled space with timber floors, cane pendants and soft mint-green banquettes. The menu leans into the lake and ocean on its doorstep, with a seafood-inspired offering balanced by modern Australian favourites built around quality local produce.

Open for daily buffet breakfast and an elegant evening service, Pelicans is equally at home for a long, leisurely meal with a glass of crisp white or a quiet dinner before retiring upstairs. Service is warm and unhurried, the wine list is thoughtfully chosen, and the atmosphere walks the line beautifully between special-occasion and easy-going coastal dining.
