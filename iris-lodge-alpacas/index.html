An award-winning, eco-certified alpaca farm in Jilliby where 145 gentle alpacas welcome small groups for hand-feeding, brunches and unhurried country mornings.

Book

One hundred and forty-five alpacas. One very good morning.

Iris Lodge is the kind of place you leave grinning involuntarily. Run by genuine alpaca obsessives, the farm has built its reputation on small groups, ethical animal care and an accessible, all-ages welcome that suits prams and wheelchairs as easily as it suits curious adults.

Choose a meet-and-greet for an hour with the herd, or stay longer for Brunch with Alpacas served paddock-side. It is calm, photogenic, slightly surreal and worth the gentle drive inland.
