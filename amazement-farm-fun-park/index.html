Amazement
Farm & Fun Park

A sprawling Central Coast farm and fun park where hedge mazes, friendly animals and Aussie wildlife share one unforgettable day out in Wyong Creek.

Book

Mazes, mobs and mischief in the Yarramalong Valley.

Tucked into the green hills of the Yarramalong Valley, Amazement Farm & Fun Park brings together three hand-grown hedge mazes, native wildlife encounters and a working farmyard on one wonderfully eccentric property. It is the rare attraction that delivers genuine surprises for both restless kids and quietly curious adults.

Expect bottle-feeding lambs, eye-to-eye moments with kangaroos and wombats, pedal carts, jumping pillows and that signature maze-solving sense of achievement. Pack a picnic, wear closed shoes and plan to lose a happy half-day to it.
