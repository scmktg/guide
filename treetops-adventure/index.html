Treetops
Adventure

NSW's biggest high-ropes park, strung through the towering eucalypts of Ourimbah State Forest, with flying foxes, bridges and challenges for every nerve level.

Book

Into the canopy, on your own steam.

Treetops Adventure turns a patch of Central Coast forest into a vertical playground of zip lines, swinging logs and rope bridges that rise through the canopy. With courses graded from cruisy family loops to genuinely heart-thumping black runs, it scales beautifully to whoever turns up.

Strap into a continuous-belay harness, listen carefully to the safety briefing, then disappear into the trees for two hours of self-paced climbing. Great for groups, sneaky for first dates and surprisingly meditative once you find your rhythm.

Guest perk

A complimentary commemorative photo download with each adult climb.
