About

A garden, a still, and a very good gin.

Distillery Botanica is the kind of place that quietly raises the bar for a region. Founder Philip Moore grows many of the botanicals on site, and the resulting gins and liqueurs are pleasingly idiosyncratic, with a signature Murraya bloom note running through the range.

Settle into the cellar door for a guided tasting flight, wander the gardens between sips, or book in for a distil-your-own gin masterclass. It is grown-up, unfussy and genuinely lovely on a slow afternoon.
