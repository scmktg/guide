01. About

Low and slow, Coast style.

Cue & Crew has been smoking meat low and slow on the Central Coast since 2017, building a signature flavour over slow-burning iron bark. Expect tender brisket, fall-apart ribs and proper pulled pork, plus the all-you-can-eat midweek nights that have built a loyal local following.

The room is loud, friendly and family-friendly, with weekend live music from local artists keeping the energy up. It is built for groups, big appetites and one more round of sides.
