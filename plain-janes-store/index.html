Long Jetty · 15 min from the hotel

Plain
Janes Store

A light-filled Long Jetty boutique stocking effortless women's wear, kids' pieces and homewares from local and Australian-made labels, curated by two sisters.

Visit website

Simple, stylish, every day.

Plain Janes is the brainchild of fabric-loving sisters and Central Coast locals who have been shaping Long Jetty's main street since 2015. The racks are tightly edited: easy, wearable women's pieces, plus a thoughtful selection of kids' clothing, accessories and homewares.

The store is white-walled and bright, the kind of browse that feels like a treat rather than a chore. Pop in between brunch and the beach for a piece you will actually wear back home.

Guest perk

Ten percent off any full-priced item in store.
