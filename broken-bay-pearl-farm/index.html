The Farm

Twenty thousand oysters drift below the surface of Broken Bay, each one quietly growing a single Akoya pearl.

The Hawkesbury is one of the few estuaries in the world clean enough and cold enough for Akoya. It's why the pearls grown here glow with a particular silvery lustre — softer than South Sea, more luminous than freshwater.

Every tour ends with a live harvest. You'll see a pearl lifted from its shell for the first time, still wet, exactly as our farmers do it every morning. Tours leave from our shoreside cellar door at Mooney Mooney, fifty-five minutes north of the Sydney Harbour Bridge.

Bring a hat. The light off the water is unreasonable.
