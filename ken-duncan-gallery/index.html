One of Australia's largest privately owned photographic galleries, showcasing Ken Duncan's sweeping panoramic landscapes a few minutes from Terrigal in Erina Heights.

Visit website

The wide brown land, beautifully framed.

Ken Duncan's purpose-built gallery is a quietly remarkable space, with high ceilings, generous lighting and walls hung with the panoramic landscapes that have come to define how Australia sees itself. The scale of the prints rewards an unhurried visit.

Take your time through the viewing rooms and the theatre, then settle into the on-site cafe to look back over your favourites. It suits art lovers, road-trippers and anyone who enjoys big country seen through a thoughtful lens.
