About

Live music in a restored cinema.

Behind the restored Savoy facade is a buzzy little venue that doubles as neighbourhood bar, music room and event space. The kitchen turns out Napoli-style pizzas, charcuterie and cheese boards, while the bar leans into natural wine, low-sulphur drops and creative cocktails.

Trivia on Wednesdays, open mic Thursdays and live music through the weekend keep the calendar humming. It is the sort of room you wander into for one drink and leave three hours later.
