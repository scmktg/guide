About

Above the dunes at Soldiers Beach.

Tucked into the top floor of Soldiers Beach Surf Club, Dunes by Dish makes the most of one of the Coast's best uninterrupted ocean outlooks. The kitchen leans into local seafood, tapas plates and elevated pub classics, all designed for long lunches that drift into sunset.

Inside is breezy and casual, with timber, light and that ever-changing blue horizon doing most of the decorating. It suits a slow Sunday with friends as easily as a celebratory dinner with a view.
