Wyong Milk
Factory

A historic 1920s dairy factory reborn as a riverside precinct of cafe, tavern, cheese makers, chocolate makers and a playground for the kids.

Visit website

A century-old factory, still feeding the Coast.

Built by local dairy farmers in the 1920s, the Wyong Milk Factory is now a riverside precinct stitched together from a relaxed cafe, a family tavern, artisan cheese and chocolate makers and a generous playground. It is the rare venue that can absorb a whole afternoon without effort.

The Tavern handles pub classics, burgers, pizzas and a Thai menu alongside local beers and live music. The Cafe leans breakfast and lunch with kid-friendly options and plenty of room to spread out by the river.

Guest perk

A complimentary kids meal with any adult main at the Tavern.
