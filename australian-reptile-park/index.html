A much-loved Somersby wildlife park where venomous snake milking, croc shows and koala cuddles fund Australia's only antivenom programme of its kind.

Scales, fangs and the country's bravest keepers.

The Reptile Park has been a Central Coast institution for decades, and it still hits the sweet spot between proper zoo and proper adventure. The keeper-led shows are genuinely lively, and the spider and snake venom programmes here save Australian lives every year.

Plan your day around the show schedule, meet Hugo the croc, get nose-to-nose with a Galapagos tortoise and finish at the dingoes. Strollers and wheelchairs roll easily; bring a hat and a sense of humour.
