Easy lakeside drinking and dining at The Beachie.

Beachie Bar and Bistro is the fun, easy-going heart of The Beachcomber — a lakeside bar and bistro built for long lunches, sunset drinks and shared plates with friends. Pull up to the veranda overlooking the water and settle in with a tropical cocktail, a cold local beer or something from a tightly curated wine list.

The kitchen turns out crowd-pleasing pub classics done well alongside crispy wood-fired pizzas, fresh salads and generous share plates. With its breezy coastal fit-out, regular live entertainment and family-friendly atmosphere, Beachie Bar and Bistro captures everything locals love about The Beachie — and is the perfect first stop after check-in.
