Self-drive boat hire on the calm, shallow waters of Tuggerah Lake, with skipper-free vessels, fishing gear on tap and no licence required.

About

On the lake, no licence required.

Bateau Tuggerah is built around the easy pleasure of an afternoon on the water. The fleet is set up for first-timers and families: stable, simple to handle and ready to go with bait, tackle and safety gear sorted at the dock.

Pack a picnic, point the bow toward a quiet shoreline and let the day unwind. The lake is calm, the wildlife is generous and you do not need a licence to take the wheel.
