Cantina

Baja-inspired Mexican cooking, salt-rim margaritas and warm cantina colour on the northern Central Coast, a short coastal drive from Terrigal.

Visit website

Baja flavours by the coast.

MexiCoast Cantina brings the sun-baked flavours of the Baja Peninsula to the Central Coast, with flame-grilled tacos, slow-cooked fillings and a tequila list that runs deep into mezcal territory. Everything is built around bright, generous plates designed to be shared across the table.

The room hums with cantina warmth: hand-painted touches, a busy bar shaking salt-rimmed margaritas, and a soundtrack that nudges you into holiday mode. It is the kind of dinner you settle into for a second round.

Guest perk

A complimentary margarita with any main meal.
